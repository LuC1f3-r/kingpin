## Challenge
Legacy retail districts in Neo-Shanghai relied on static content loops that ignored live traffic, emotion, and purchasing signals.

## Approach
We orchestrated a swarm of low-poly lidar nodes that continually fed a differentiable brand model. Next.js Edge streams piped heuristics straight into the signage stack. GSAP scenes choreographed the volumetric UI, while Lenis governed people flow in digital twins.

## Outcome
* 38% uplift in contextual conversions within 6 weeks.
* 4.1 second faster dwell-time capture thanks to predictive media preloading.
* Neural governance layer ready for future reinforcement learning drops.
